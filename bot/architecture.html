Health Tracker

Sleep, daytime watch strain, nutrition, and evening meds in one place.

Inputs
Oura + Garmin + Cronometer + Meds
APIs, CSV, and @health_tracker_bot
->
Sync
sync_to_render.py
Pulls APIs + Telegram state into sleep_vault.json
->
Output
Health Dashboard
Oura | Garmin | Nutrition | Merged | Insights

Data Sources

  • Oura Ring: sleep score, deep/REM, HRV, wakeups
  • Garmin Watch: daytime strain, steps, workouts
  • Cronometer: daily calories, macros, micronutrients
  • Telegram (@health_tracker_bot): meds, time taken, nicotine, alcohol, hunger

Dashboard Tabs

  • Oura: sleep & evening med timing
  • Garmin: watch vitals & workouts
  • Nutrition: Cronometer macros & micros
  • Merged & Insights: combined signals and auto takeaways

Weightlifting Tracker

Log lifts on Telegram and track strength progress on the dashboard.

Input
Telegram (@weightlifting-tracker-bot)
Sets, reps, and weights from your phone
->
Sync
sync_to_render.py
Merges cloud logs into workout_data.json + lifting_vault.json
->
Output
Weightlifting Dashboard
Exercise history, targets, and volume trends

How It Works

  • Text your lift updates to @weightlifting-tracker-bot at the gym
  • Render saves them immediately to cloud state
  • sync all pulls them into local workout_data.json and rebuilds the dashboard

Key Files

  • data/workout_data.json (local source of truth)
  • dashboard.html (Weightlifting Tracker tab)
  • bot/sync_to_render.py -> lifting_vault.json

Executive Assistant (Job Search)

Finds matching jobs, filters out bad fits and dead links, and builds your Google Sheet + Cover Letters.

1. Search
Job Boards & ATS
21-day sweep across LinkedIn, Indeed, BuiltIn, and company boards
->
2. Filter
scout_jobs.py
$140k+, US Remote / Austin Hybrid, zero 5-day onsite, live link check
->
3. AI Review
Fit + Recruiter Match
Checks real skills (SQL/BI/dbt/ETL) and finds recruiter/HM
->
4. Output
Sheet + Cover Letters
Google Sheet rows, Word/PDF cover letters, and Interview Prep

How Job Filtering Works

  • Targets Analytics Engineering, SQL/Warehouse Data Engineering, BI, and Senior Analytics
  • Blocks dead/closed links and aggregator fallbacks
  • Blocks roles needing Databricks, Spark/Kafka/AWS-Glue infra, RDF/OWL, or consulting/IB pedigree

Google Sheet & Cover Letters

  • Adds approved jobs to Google Sheet as "Queued to apply"
  • Creates Word + PDF cover letters for every Queued job
  • Moves Applied/Interviewing PDFs into Cover Letters (Submitted)
  • Includes "Decided not to apply, bad fit", "Job link broken", and "Job posting no longer available"
