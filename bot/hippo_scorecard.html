Result 1 - Optimization Plan

Recommended Channel Actions

Protect Builder's conversion while balancing CAT, unlock National's bind rate, and tighten AgencyLink going forward.

1. BuilderProtect & Diversify
  • Maintain embedded point-of-sale closing integration driving 47.7% bind rate.
  • Steer new community launches outside TX (57%) and FL (21%) to bring down 83% High/Very-High CAT share.
  • Tighten pre-closing escrow & inspection handoffs to trim 7-day closing cancels.
2. NationalLift Conversion
  • Test targeted pricing / quoting adjustments on unbound shoppers (currently +12% higher quoted premium).
  • Lifting bind rate from 4.1% to 6.0% adds ~$1.8M+ bound_premium with zero new traffic.
  • Preserve strong 79.6% Low/Mod CAT mix and low 21.6% loss ratio.
3. AgencyLinkTighten & Prune
  • Stop taking new quotes on home_age_years >15-20, insurance_score <680, and High CAT going forward.
  • Deactivate ~270 inactive/1-policy tail sub-agencies; focus on top ~35-50 producers.
  • Set 90-day probation (<10% cancel, <45% LR) before full channel exit.
