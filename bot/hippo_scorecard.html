Result 1 - Optimization Plan

Recommended Channel Actions

Protect Builder's conversion while balancing catastrophe risk, unlock National's bind rate, and tighten AgencyLink going forward.

1. BuilderProtect & Diversify
  • Protect the closing-flow integration driving a 48% bind rate.
  • Steer new builder launches outside TX (57%) and FL (21%) to bring down 83% High/Very-High catastrophe risk.
  • Tighten pre-closing escrow & inspection handoffs to trim 7-day cancels.
2. NationalLift Conversion
  • Test targeted pricing adjustments on unbound shoppers (currently +12% pricier across nearly 58k quotes).
  • Lifting bind rate from 4.1% to 6.0% adds ~$1.8M bound premium with zero new traffic.
  • Preserve 80% Low/Moderate catastrophe mix and low 22% loss ratio.
3. AgencyLinkTighten & Prune
  • Stop quoting older homes (>15-20 yrs), Insurance Scores <680, and High catastrophe risk going forward.
  • Deactivate ~276 zero/1-bound tail sub-agencies; focus on top ~35-50 active partners (protecting $720k clean book).
  • Set a 90-day performance review (<10% cancel, <45% LR) before any channel exit.
Result 2 - Next Partner Strategy

Ideal Future Producer Profile

Combine Builder's closing conversion & underwriting quality with National's geographic and catastrophe balance.

Keep From BuilderConversion
  • Built right into the home closing flow (~30-48% bind rate).
  • Tight, manageable partner count (<15 sub-agencies).
  • Strong Insurance Score (680+) and newer homes (<15 yrs old).
Keep From NationalRisk Balance
  • 75-80% Low / Moderate catastrophe risk mix.
  • Geographic diversity outside TX/FL storm corridors.
  • One direct partner connection instead of hundreds of scattered offices.
Target Partner FitWho to Sign
  • National / Regional Mortgage Lenders & Digital Closing Platforms (built into loan lock/escrow).
  • Midwest / Mid-Atlantic Homebuilders (lower-catastrophe new construction).
  • Curated Digital Agencies with upfront Insurance Score & home-age filters.
